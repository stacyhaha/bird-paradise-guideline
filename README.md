# Bird Paradise Guide - Animal Crossing Style

A beautiful, interactive bird guide inspired by Animal Crossing, featuring 64 birds from 10 different zones at Singapore's Bird Paradise.

## Features

### 🎮 Animal Crossing Style Design

- Soft, pastel color scheme with rounded corners
- Card-based layout similar to Animal Crossing's catalog
- Smooth animations and hover effects
- Found/Not Found status indicators

### 🦅 Comprehensive Bird Database

- **64 birds** from 10 unique zones
- **Bilingual names** (English and Chinese)
- **Detailed information** including:
  - Lifespan
  - Diet
  - Habitat
  - Geographic range
  - IUCN conservation status
  - Fun facts

### 📸 Photo Upload System

- Upload your own photos of birds you've spotted
- **Screenshot feature**: You can screenshot birds from the official website and upload them
- Photos are stored locally in your browser
- Uploaded photos replace default images
- Perfect for documenting your Bird Paradise visit

### ✅ Collection Tracking

- Mark birds as "Found" or "Not Found"
- Visual indicators on bird cards
- Persistent storage using browser's local storage
- Track your progress through all 64 birds

### 🔍 Smart Search & Filtering

- Search by bird name (English or Chinese)
- Filter by zone
- Quick access to specific bird information

## How to Use

### 1. Browse Birds

- Click on any zone card to see birds from that area
- Use the search bar to find specific birds
- Click on any bird card to view detailed information

### 2. Mark Birds as Found

- Click on a bird card to open its detail page
- Click "Mark as Found" to add it to your collection
- Found birds will show a golden checkmark (✓) on the main page

### 3. Upload Photos

- Open any bird's detail page
- Click "Choose File" to select a photo
- Click "Upload Photo" to save your image
- Your photo will replace the default image

### 4. Track Your Progress

- Found birds are highlighted with golden borders
- Checkmarks appear on found bird cards
- Your progress is automatically saved
